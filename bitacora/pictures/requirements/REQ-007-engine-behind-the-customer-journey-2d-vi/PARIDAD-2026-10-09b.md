# REQ-007 parity report, refresh: engine 1.2.2 against stored Python, 28 catalogue ideas

## 0. Summary

- **Counts now match Python in total.** On the 26 ideas the engine fully builds, it counts 2,173 balloons against
  Python's 2,170 (×1.00). In PARIDAD-2026-10-09 (engine 1.1.0) it counted 3,278 (×1.51).
- **Per idea, the spread is still wide:** 0.77 (arches 01, 14, which keep the official 5 quartets per metre) to 1.40
  (31). 18 of the 26 ideas are within ±15 %. The eight outside are 01, 02, 14 (classic arches), 09, 25, 12, 30, 31.
- **The organic R-5 filler dropped** from 56–63 % of the count to 20–28 %, the same share Python uses (20–28 %). The
  golden test caps it at 34 %.
- **Prices fell further, and the engine's counts now cost more than Python's counts.** Engine `python` policy over
  the stored Python price: 0.70 in total (0.65 in 09), median 0.68 per idea. 21 ideas are below Python, 5 at or above
  (08 is exactly 1.00; 12, 13, 29 and 31 are above). Priced with Python's own counts under the same policy, the engine's
  counts now cost **1.18×** (on 24 ideas), up from 1.06× in 09. This is a size-mix effect, see §4.
- **Two data issues to check before quoting:** ideas 30 and 31 carry the same stored Python price ($57,603), and the
  09 price ratios for ideas 09 and 25 still lack a crosswalk entry (crema, multicolor). See §5.

## 4. What the price moves mean

1. **Packaging still dominates.** Stored Python plans buy the allowlisted variants, mostly 50-packs. Quoting the same
   counts with the standard presentations (D-026) removes about a third of the price. This is unchanged from 09 and is
   a pricing decision, not an engine property.
2. **Counting now adds about 18 %, not 5 %.** Same policy, Python's counts against the engine's: 1.18× on 24 ideas (09:
   1.06× on 23). The engine's balloons are now bigger on average. The 1.2.0 spread of premium colours across sizes
   moved colour units out of R-5, and those are R-9 and R-12 balloons with a higher unit price. This is the likely
   cause for 04, 05 and 17 (count −24 %, price +28 to +46 %) and 12 (+58 % price while its count fell 19 %). **Not yet
   verified:** the material-value column of 09 §1 was not recomputed here.
3. **Engine price falls where the count falls.** 25 (−48 %), 18 (−37 %), 07 (−30 %) and 28 (−21 %) have the counts
   reduced by the 1.2.0 caps and by the classic garland now using R-12.
4. **06 is cheap for the wrong reason.** The engine's 44 balloons cost 24,350 against 80,096 stored, but Python's own
   44 cost the same 24,350 under the same policy. The gap is packaging, as in point 1.

## 5. Data issues found (not engine defects, to check before a quote)

- **30 and 31 share the stored Python price, $57,603,** both as `pythonGuardado` and in the 09 report. Their Python
  counts (60 and 75) and the policy prices (23,396 and 23,565) differ, so the stored price looks copied. Check the
  `planes-ideas` / analysis source before quoting either.
- **Ideas 09 and 25** have stored Python lines with variants the crosswalk does not carry (crema, multicolor). Their
  Python-counts price cannot be computed on this snapshot, so ratios for them compare the engine with the stored price
  only.
- **Idea 27 (aro with a partial hoop) and idea 03's centre table** are reported `noRepresentable`, as designed. The
  parity script reports zero for them and the price is not computed. Idea 03 is therefore not priced by the engine; its stored Python price stands.
