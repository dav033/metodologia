## How, within the licences (D-135 stands)
- Bencho licences are UNKNOWN and React Bits Free carries a Commons Clause: do NOT fetch or copy their code. Write every entry **from scratch (clean-room)** from the inventory's description and the SPEC's naming (§10.3 names for Bencho; R10 origin `own (inspired by <source>)`), as static HTML/CSS (+ the small optional `kit.js` or a per-component inline script) that works offline in a sandboxed iframe. Where an entry is impossible or pointless in a static mockup (needs WebGL, a video, a heavy effect), implement an honest light approximation and mark it `approximation` in the manifest, or list it as `excluded` with a written reason, like david-ui's gaps page does. Never fake: every entry's status must be true.
- Animate UI enhancements are motion/feedback on top of existing shadcn controls: implement them as variants/states of the existing component snippets (e.g. Button with press feedback, Tabs with animated indicator, Dialog with enter/exit, Toast stack), driven by the three motion profiles (subtle/natural/expressive) and fully disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Everything stays AA in both themes (extend `contrast.py` and the audit to the new entries; decorative effects/backgrounds must keep any text above them at AA and must show their static fallback under reduced motion).
