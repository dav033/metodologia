# E2E on Maros: findings (what is wrong or missing in the tool)

Format: `F-NN · severity (high/med/low) · where · what happened · proposed fix`. Observed facts only; fixes are proposals.

## Before launch
- F-01 · med · `handoff` risk gate · The gate is a keyword scan of SPEC/PLAN. A safety constraint that says "never touch production" and an out-of-scope line mentioning "billing" or "notify users" force `needs_approval`, exactly as a risky plan would. · Proposal: ignore matches inside "Out of scope" and "Constraints" sections, or require the keyword in a task line, and show the matched sentence.
- F-02 · high · `handoff` / `launch` · Neither exposes `--isolation`; only `run` does and the default is `run.isolation=off`. A plain `handoff` therefore runs sessions directly in the registered repo path. `maros-next` has 213 uncommitted files of the owner's own work; unattended sessions would write into that tree. · Proposal: `handoff` should refuse (or default to worktree) when a target repo is dirty or on a feature branch, and accept `--isolation`.
- F-03 · med · `req new` · Leaves SPEC in `status: clarifying`; the HQ must flip it by hand. · Proposal: a `spec ready` command or auto-flip when the plan is written.
- F-04 · low · workspace hints · `workspace show` prints `hint: ~/maros-next` while the repos are in `~/dev/maros/...`; the real paths come from another resolver, so the output misleads. · Proposal: print the resolved path.
- F-05 · med · base selection · Both repos are on feature branches with unmerged work (`feat/req-003-...`, `feat/req-001-...`); there is no prompt or flag to pick the base branch for the worktrees, so the new work would be based on whatever is checked out. · Proposal: `--base <branch>` and a warning that names the branch.
- F-06 · med · relay hook · At 11:40 (the reset minute of the 5 h window) the UserPromptSubmit hook still told the session "100% used, resets 11:40, run relay handoff" while `globalusage` already showed 0.0% for the same account one minute later. Acting on it would have handed a healthy session to another account. · Proposal: have the hook re-measure (or ignore a reset time that is in the past or within the polling skew) before demanding a handoff.
- F-07 · low · usage panel · Two of four pool accounts (codex-1, codex-luis) were at 100% of their 5 h window when the marathon started; the pool still lists them. Check in the run log that the router skipped them.

## During the run (REQ-005, marathon with `run.isolation=worktree`)
- F-08 · high · isolated run, partial milestone · S1 (claude-personal sonnet xhigh) finished T1 (commit `8551cee`, 42 tests, tsc and eslint clean) and then hit the 5 h window during T2. `settle_unit` returns before integrating when not every task of the unit is done, and the candidate was journaled as `scope_unchecked: scope declarations are missing` and rejected. A new worktree was allocated from the base and another provider redoes T1 from scratch; the verified commit stays orphaned on `wt/M1-50d08786`. · Proposal: integrate the tasks that are done (or resume in the same worktree / base the retry on the previous candidate); never treat "unit incomplete" as "scope missing".
- F-09 · high · router and quota · After S1 the router sent S2 to `codex-1` and S3 to `codex-luis`, both at 100% of their 5 h window according to `globalusage` taken at the same minute. S2 burned 86 s and learned "cooling until 11:54" only from the failure; S3 failed in 4 s. · Proposal: consult the measured quota (`usage poll`) before choosing an account, and skip accounts with a known exhaustion.
- F-10 · med · limit detection · S3 failed with `ERROR: Your workspace is out of credits. Add credits to continue.` (Codex). It is not matched as a usage limit, so it was recorded as a plain `failed` attempt: T1 attempts=2 and the escalation ladder advanced for a reason unrelated to the task. · Proposal: add "out of credits" to the limit patterns and never count it as an attempt.
- F-11 · med · result summary · For S2 and S3 the stored `summary` is a fragment of the prompt echo (`... returns a compact checked result, not a transcript ...`, a code excerpt) instead of the provider's error line, which made the run log misleading. · Proposal: when the exit is non-zero take the last ERROR line, not the first 300 chars of merged output.
- F-12 · med · tier choice · The first milestone session ran on `claude-personal sonnet xhigh` although the supervisor was started with `--model haiku` and the plan has a single `level: architecture` task in M3; the milestone's tier was raised because its tasks are architecture-heavy. It is not stated in the launch output which tier a milestone gets and why. · Proposal: print the tier decision (task, reason) in the run log.
- F-13 · low · relay hook · see F-06; additionally the same minute the 5 h window of the HQ session reset, the supervisor's own Claude session hit its limit: both were the same account, so HQ and executor compete for one 5 h window.
- O-01 · works · S4 (antigravity gemini-3.8-flash-medium, 12:04, ~29 min in) produced in a clean worktree two task commits with their specs: T1 `4de4bcf` (catalog with label/risk/implies, templates) and T2 `bfd0340` (22 files, +1691/-117: guard-rails service, impact service, duplicate role DTO, controller/service/repository changes, tests). One commit per task, scoped to the plan. Quality still to be judged by the M1 audit and by reading the diff.
- F-14 · high · quality telemetry · 8 of 8 supervised audits recorded so far (maros-lab, pictures) produced no defect row: `audits_unreported == audits_done`. The audit prompt asks for `task done --findings N`, but no audit has ever reported, so `findings_per_audited_task` and `rework` have no data and the only quality signal the router could use does not exist. · Proposal: make the supervisor refuse to accept an audit `done` without `--findings` (or re-ask once), and show the gap in `metrics`.
- Telemetry v2 shipped (commit below): usage of cut sessions (partial), provider-loss class, provider error excerpt, change size, origin (`metrics --origin real`), audit-report coverage and `success_rate_excl_loss`. All additive; no decision reads them. The supervisor of REQ-005 was started before this change, so its rows carry the old schema.
- F-15 · low · integration verify · The combined verification of milestone M1 chains the three task verifies (`npx jest src/common/auth` && `npx jest src/modules/users src/common` && `npx jest src/modules/users`), so the same suites run two or three times (6+ minutes at the time of writing, still running). · Proposal: de-duplicate overlapping paths or run one `jest` with the union of the paths.
- F-16 · med · session restart · When this HQ conversation was restarted the two background `watch` jobs were reported as stopped. The supervisor (tmux) was unaffected, which is the right design, but nothing told the new HQ session that the run was healthy; it had to be rediscovered from RUN.json, tmux and the integration journal. · Proposal: the SessionStart context should include "active runs: REQ-005 (maros) running, last beat 12:26, verifying M1".
- F-17 · high · integration worktree has no dependencies · The integration worktree (`REQ-005-integration-*`) is created from the base commit without `node_modules`. The combined verification runs `npx jest ...` there, and `npx` stops at `Need to install the following packages: jest@30.5.2 / Ok to proceed? (y)`, waiting forever on stdin. Nothing times out: it hung 12+ minutes (S4's 51-minute milestone was waiting to be accepted). The only signals were a heartbeat that stopped ("supervisor may have died") and a `ps` listing. · Proposal: provision dependencies in integration worktrees the same way as session worktrees (or run verify with `npx --no-install` / `CI=1` / stdin closed), and give verify a wall-clock timeout.
- F-18 · high · failure handling cost · When the verification finally failed (I killed the hung `npx`), the supervisor rolled the integration branch back, allocated a new worktree from the base and relaunched the whole milestone on **opus xhigh** (S5) as an ordinary failed attempt (T1 attempts=3, tier 2), discarding S4's three verified commits. An environment failure was treated as the agent's failure and escalated the tier. · Proposal: classify "verification could not run" (command not found, missing deps, timeout, interactive prompt) as an infrastructure error: keep the candidate, do not count an attempt, do not escalate, and surface it.
- F-19 · med · manual recovery · To avoid paying for the redo I stopped the run (`run-control stop`), reset the new worktree to S4's candidate `8b46730` with `git reset --hard`, and resumed with `run --resume`. There is no supported command to "adopt this branch as the milestone's candidate". · Proposal: `run adopt <task|milestone> <branch>` that re-verifies and integrates an existing candidate.
- F-20 · low · heartbeat · During a long combined verification the supervisor writes no heartbeat, so `watch` reports "it may have died" after 12 min although the process is alive. · Proposal: beat while verify runs, and say what is running.
- O-02 · works · After the recovery (F-17..F-19) S6 (opus xhigh, 11 min) finished M1 on the adopted candidate and added review fixes (`56cf2dc`: keep role changes when the audit insert fails, allow clearing a description); integration accepted in 9 s; `M1-audit` (antigravity flash-low, 3 min) re-ran the three jest groups plus tsc/eslint, checked R1-R4 one by one against the contract, confirmed no SQL was executed, and deferred two follow-ups as pending items. This is the first supervised audit with a defect row (`defects-2026-10.jsonl`), so F-14 is not universal: the audit prompt can work. Open question: why did the 8 earlier audits not report `--findings`?
- O-03 · note · M1 took ~1h20 of wall time and three providers (sonnet xhigh partial, gemini medium 51 min, opus xhigh 11 min) for what is three tasks; most of it was recoverable overhead (F-08, F-09, F-17, F-18).
